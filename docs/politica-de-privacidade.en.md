# Privacy Policy — Jam Cards - YGO!

Last updated: October 29, 2026.

*This is a translation of the original Portuguese version. In case of any difference, the Portuguese version prevails.*

This policy explains which personal data the **Jam Cards - YGO!** app ("App") processes, for what, with whom it is shared and what your rights are, under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, "LGPD").

By creating an account or using the App's online features, you declare that you have read and agree with this policy and with the [Terms of Use](termos-de-uso.md).

## 1. Who is responsible

The App is an independent and free project, maintained by its creators and developers ("Developers"), who act as controllers of the data described here.

Contact for privacy matters: **[contact e-mail]**.

The App is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Konami. Yu-Gi-Oh! is a registered trademark of Konami.

## 2. What data we process

We collect the **minimum necessary** for the App to work.

| Data | When | What for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E-mail | at sign-up | identify the account, confirm the sign-up and recover the password |
| Username | at sign-up | identify you in the App; it is **public** |
| Password | at sign-up | access to the account; it is stored only as a *hash* (we cannot read it) |
| Combos, decks and author name | when you save them | store and display what you created; build and improve the App's database (see below) |
| AI settings (provider, model, address and API key) | **only if you tick** "save in my account" | use the same AI on other computers; only you have access |
| Technical logs (IP address, date and time of access, device type) | when using online features | security, abuse prevention and operation of the service |

**We do not collect** full name, tax ID, address, phone, location, payment data or sensitive data (art. 5, II, of the LGPD). We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

**Data that stays only on your device** and is not sent to the Developers: the card database and cached images, App preferences, the TCG API key (when provided) and the AI API key, unless you choose to save it in the account.

**Decks and combos in the App's database:** under the [Terms of Use](termos-de-uso.md), the decks and combos you save (public or private) may be used to build and improve the App's database, such as card usage statistics and combo calculation and checking. Private ones are only used in aggregate form or without identifying you. This use is limited to decks and combos; no other account data is used for this purpose.

**AI API key saved in the account:** it stays in the database, protected by access rules so that only your account can read it. It is only used by the App to call the AI provider you chose. Saving it is optional: if you prefer, keep the key only on the device, untick the option and it is deleted from the account. We recommend using a key with a spending limit on the provider's dashboard.

## 3. Legal bases

- **Performance of contract** (art. 7, V): create and maintain your account and the content you save.
- **Legitimate interest** (art. 7, IX): security, fraud and abuse prevention, App improvement and use of decks and combos in the App's database (in aggregate form or without identifying you, for private ones). You can object to this use through the contact in item 1.
- **Consent** (art. 7, I): publish combos and decks marked as public, with the author name you choose. You can make the content private or delete it at any time.
- **Compliance with legal obligation** (art. 7, II): keep access logs when the law requires (Brazilian Internet Civil Framework, Law No. 12,965/2014).

## 4. Who we share with

We do not sell or rent personal data. It is only processed by service providers needed for the App to work (processors, art. 39 of the LGPD):

- **Supabase** (database and authentication hosting). The servers are in the United States.
- **GitHub** (distribution of the App and public card data). It does not receive your account data.

We may also share data by court order or request from a competent authority.

**International transfer (art. 33):** since Supabase hosts the data outside Brazil, the transfer relies on the provider's contractual guarantees and is necessary to perform the service you requested.

## 5. Public content

Combos and decks marked as **public** can be seen by anyone, together with the author name and username. Do not include personal data (yours or other people's) in titles, notes or the author name.

## 6. How long we keep data

We keep the data while your account exists. When you ask for your account to be deleted, we delete the account, the profile, the combos and the decks, except what the law requires us to keep. Access logs are kept for the legal period of 6 months (art. 15 of the Brazilian Internet Civil Framework).

## 7. Security

We adopt reasonable technical measures to protect the data:

- password only as a *hash*;
- encrypted communication (HTTPS);
- access rules in the database, so that each person can only change what is theirs;
- minimal data collection.

No system connected to the internet is completely invulnerable, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If a security incident occurs that may cause relevant risk or damage, we will notify the affected data subjects and the National Data Protection Authority (ANPD), as provided in art. 48 of the LGPD.

## 8. Limitation of liability

The App is offered **free of charge and as is**, with no guarantee of continuous, error-free or fault-free operation. **To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law**, the Developers **are not liable** for damages resulting from:

1. **Exclusive fault of the data subject** (art. 43, III, of the LGPD), such as a weak password or one reused in other services, sharing the password, access from a compromised device or network, or lack of care with one's own e-mail.
2. **Exclusive act of third parties** (art. 43, III, of the LGPD), such as attacks, intrusions, unauthorized access, viruses or security failures of service providers and third-party platforms (including the hosting provider), that did not result from a breach of data protection law by the Developers.
3. **Information the user made public**, such as combos, decks, author name and any published text.
4. **Unavailability, loss or alteration of data** caused by acts of God, force majeure or failures of third-party services.
5. **Misuse of the App** contrary to the Terms of Use.

Where there is liability, it is limited to **direct damages actually proven** and resulting from a breach of data protection law by the Developers themselves (art. 42 of the LGPD). Lost profits, indirect damages and loss of opportunity are excluded, except where the law forbids this exclusion.

Nothing in this section removes rights the law guarantees and that cannot be waived, especially those of the LGPD and the Consumer Protection Code.

## 9. Your rights (art. 18 of the LGPD)

You can, at any time:

- confirm whether we process your data and access it;
- correct incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
- ask for anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data or data processed in non-compliance;
- ask for data portability;
- ask for deletion of data processed with consent and revoke that consent;
- know with whom we share your data;
- file a complaint with the ANPD.

To exercise these rights, write to **[contact e-mail]**. We answer within 15 days.

## 10. Children and adolescents

The App is not intended for children under 12, who must not create an account. Adolescents between 12 and 18 must use the App with the knowledge and authorization of their parents or guardians.
